SCORIDIUM SUPPORT

How can we help?

Common account and team setup questions.

I can't sign in

Check your email and password. If you recently registered, confirm your email using the link sent to your inbox. Check spam or junk as well.

I forgot my password

Use the password reset form to request a new password. The reset link must be opened in the same Scoridium site.

Reset password

How do I create a team?

Sign in, select Create Team on My Teams, choose School or Club, and enter the team identity, division, season and colours.

My team isn't appearing

Check that you're using the account assigned to that team's membership, then refresh My Teams.
